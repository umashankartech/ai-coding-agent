AUTHENTICATION

The application uses JWT authentication.
The login controller validates the user's credentials.
After successful login, an access token is generated.
The authentication middleware verifies the JWT token.
The middleware attaches the authenticated user to the request.


CART

The cart controller handles cart operations.
Users can add products to their cart.
Users can update product quantities.
Users can remove products from their cart.
The cart summary calculates the total price.


PRODUCTS

The product controller handles product operations.
Products can be searched by name.
Products can be filtered by category.
Products are retrieved according to the selected pincode.
